The Adventure (v0.24)

Three Acts — Phandalin, White Plume Mountain, and The Hells. Four heroes. Ten rotating bosses.

  • Soft Lanes (Breach & Flank): All 13 battle maps split into 2 or 3 tactical lanes with distinct objectives (Dark Ritual, Supply Cart, Duel Choke, Battle Shrine, Boss Throne). Win a lane and your heroes cross open bridges to flank the rest!
  • 36 Shop-Offered House Rules (6-Slot Cap): Build multiplicative party-wide engines around Class Keywords, d20 manipulation, lanes, and economy — supercharged by Multiclass (✧) shrines to beat the exponential Act 3 curve.
  • Immediate Power vs. Future Power Shop: Spend gold now on heroes & gear, or invest in Camp Rank (I → IV), higher Gold Interest (up to +12g/win), and 6 🌱 Scaling investment items that grow after every victory.
  • Challenge Rating (CR) & Combat Autopsy: Compare your live Party CR against pre-scouted Map Nodes and per-lane matchups before fighting, then inspect lane outcomes, House Rule output, AC/SR loss, and your d20 Luck Audit after every fight.
  • All 24 Heroes & 88 Canonical D&D Items Intact: 13 Class Keywords, 1-line Tier II Specializations, Tier IV Epic Boons, Opt-In Quests, Cubed AC/SR math, and 100% hands-off autobattling.
