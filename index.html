The Adventure (v0.22)

Three acts, three bosses, four heroes. Build a trigger machine.

  • Pre-Run Pack Selection (24 Heroes, 6 Packs). Enable or disable any combination of the 6 Hero Packs before your run starts. All heroes and Pack Items from enabled Packs appear in your shops across all 3 Acts.
  • Reworked Shop (No Duplicate Hunting). Two dedicated Recruit Slots always show unowned heroes, while the Upgrade Slot offers a direct Next-Tier Upgrade for an owned hero. In Acts 2 & 3, new recruits arrive pre-tiered (Tier II/III) so you can pivot cleanly.
  • Six-Attribute Rework & d20 Floors. STR adds melee Armor Pen, DEX drives Attack Speed, CON scales HP, INT speeds Charge (capped at 1 cast/sec), WIS raises your minimum natural d20 roll floor, and CHA scales buff/debuff potency.
  • Campfire Retraining & Hero Quests. Retrain any Tier II+ Path freely at Campfires, promote heroes at Training nodes, and complete each hero’s built-in Hero Quest for Signature Rewards (like Large 2×2 Sir Dane & Pillar).
  • Piggy Bank Loss Rule. Wins fill your Piggy Bank. Your first loss breaks it, pays out all saved gold, and lets you shop and retry that fight. A second loss ends the run.
