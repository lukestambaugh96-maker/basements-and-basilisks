The Adventure (v0.23)

Three Acts — Phandalin, White Plume Mountain, and The Hells. Four heroes. Ten rotating bosses.

  • 3-Act Campaign & 9-Row Constricting Map. Chart non-crossing routes through Phandalin (Klarg miniboss), White Plume Mountain (Dungeon Hazards & Vault Loot), and The Hells (Wandering Pit Fiend & Devil’s Bargains).
  • 24 Reworked Heroes & 13 Canonical Class Keywords. Clean Tier I cards with strict progressive disclosure, strictly 1-line Tier II Specializations, universal Tier IV Epic Boons (pick 1 of 3), opt-in Quest Board (❖) quests, and 12 single-class named Multiclass (✧) shrines.
  • 82 Canonical D&D Items Across 6 Tiers. 58 Universal Standard Shop items (Common → Legendary) with exact 6-attribute parity, plus 24 Unique Class-Keyword relics (2 per class) sold at the Curio Merchant (⚖).
  • Cubed AC/SR Curve, Universal Evasion & Radiant Damage. Every point of AC/SR matters inside bounded accuracy (10–20), countered by Armor/Spell Pen, rare Radiant true damage, and multiplicative Evasion.
  • Camp Shop Flow Flip & Piggy Bank. Visit the Camp Shop between fights (with Whole-Shop Freeze and an Act Boss Reminder), jump straight into Tactical Deployment on combat nodes, and rely on your Piggy Bank for one free retry on your first loss.
