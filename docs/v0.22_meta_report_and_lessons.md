# Basements & Basilisks v0.22 — Meta Simulation Archive & Design Lessons

**Snapshot Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Interactive Explorer:** Open [`tools/wiki.html`](../tools/wiki.html) (or click **Field Guide & Stat Tracker** on the title screen)  
**Machine-Readable Telemetry:** [`data/wiki-meta-stats.js`](../data/wiki-meta-stats.js) & raw simulation snapshots in [`data/v0.22-meta/`](../data/v0.22-meta/)

**Methodology:** ~6,400+ headless combat simulations and **240 end-to-end 3-Act runs** (120 pre-balance baseline + 120 post-balance verification) run directly against the live engine ([`src/03-heroes.js`](../src/03-heroes.js), [`src/04-items.js`](../src/04-items.js), [`src/05-engine.js`](../src/05-engine.js), [`src/06-adventure.js`](../src/06-adventure.js)) with deterministic seeded RNG, real front/back deployment (`autoPlace`), shop odds, map events, Hero Quests, and the Piggy Bank rule.

> **Power Score Metric:** Alongside Win Rate (`WR%`), benchmarks report a continuous **Power Score** (`0–100` / `0.000–1.000`): wins score `50 + 50 × (surviving party HP%)`; losses score `50 × (1 − remaining enemy HP%)`.

## 1. v0.22 Mistakes & Root Causes (Do Not Repeat in v0.23+)

This section documents the exact structural bugs and balance failures uncovered by the v0.22 simulations so future versions can avoid them from day one.

### Mistake 1 — Double-Scaled `u.dm²` Heals (Missing `{ raw: 1 }`)
- **What happened:** In [`heal()`](../src/05-engine.js), amounts without `{ raw: 1 }` are automatically multiplied by `src.dm`. Seven newly authored Pack 4–6 heals (*Mother Laurel*, *Kaska* ×2, *Brother Jamison*, *Saint*, *Lumen*, *Pillar*, and *Zoe*) pre-multiplied by `u.dm` **and** omitted `{ raw: 1 }`, causing Tier IV heals to scale by **`u.dm²` = `4.83×`** instead of **`2.20×`**.
- **Impact:** Packs 4–6 had 97–100% WR naked while Packs 1–3 languished at 8–50% WR.
- **Guardrail for v0.23+:** Never multiply by `u.dm` at call sites if `heal()` already applies `src.dm` (or enforce one uniform convention and lint/assert it in `tools/verify-v022.mjs`).

### Mistake 2 — Uncapped Compounding Multipliers & Zero-Cooldown Cross-Hero Triggers
- **What happened:**
  - **Zoe** (`94.4%` WR pre-patch) applied `a.dm *= 1.12` on every High/Nat 20 *Rummage* with **no stack cap**, compounding exponentially into better heals than every Cleric + huge Wards + Action Surge.
  - **Mother Laurel** (`Bountiful Vines`, `+0.240` ahead of Path 1) and **Juniper** (`Circle of the Land`, `+0.171` ahead of Path 1) fired party-wide heal/Ward pulses on every ally Smite / Wild Shape with **no internal cooldown**.
  - **Faelan** (`94.1%` WR pre-patch) looped `35%` High Charge refunds + `+20` Arcane Recovery per Ward break into a ~1 cast/sec Ward wall (plus a duplicate `u.stats.ward +=` tracking bug).
- **Guardrail for v0.23+:**
  - Every recursive combat stat multiplier (`dm`, DR, Haste, Ward) must have a hard stack cap.
  - Every cross-hero reactive trigger (`emitClassKw` / `hit` hooks) must carry an explicit `>= 1.0s` internal cooldown matching the global `MIN_CAST_INTERVAL = 1.0` philosophy.

### Mistake 3 — Sustain Overpowered Relative to Damage Combos (Infinite Stalls)
- **What happened:** Pre-balance pure offensive comps (*Pack 3*, *Burn + Rage + Ki*, *d20 Inspiration*) collapsed to **8.3% WR**, while any sustain+trigger stack hit **100% WR** and stalled indefinitely because Overtime only began at `40s` and never decayed healing or Ward.
- **Fix applied:** Moved Overtime to `28s` (`OT_STEP = 5s`) with **`−15%` Heal & Ward decay per stack (max `−60%`)**, added **`+12%` base `dm` for Damage-role heroes**, reworked **Hunter’s Mark** (`+22%` team-wide damage + `1d10+WIS`), added **Inspired Flourish** (`+25%` damage & Haste on Bardic Inspiration spend), and buffed Burn/Hex/Ki/Sneak Attack bursts.
- **Guardrail for v0.23+:** Whenever a new defensive support or healing path is added, benchmark pure offense comps vs. sustain comps head-to-head; sustain should buy time for carries, not solo-win fights by outlasting enemy DPS forever.

### Mistake 4 — Act 1 Non-Lethality & Economy Saturation Ceiling
- **What happened:** Across 120 pre-balance full runs, **0 runs died in Act 1** (`CURVE.ease[0] = 0.56` stacked with `d===1` `0.75/0.70` and `share = 0.32`). By Act 3, even Casual/Zero-Item bots cleared **68%** of runs, and competent bots routinely capped out at **4× Tier IV + 16/16 items + 3.8 completed Quests** (`181–188g` total gold; Toot alone printed `13g/fight`).
- **Fix applied:** Raised enemy scaling across Acts 1–3 (`CURVE.ease: [0.95..1.46]`, `CURVE.grow: 1.225`, `CURVE.dmgGrow: 1.28`, `bossDepth: [8.2, 13.0, 17.5]`, `eliteK: 1.26`), tightened win gold, hero prices, Campfire forage, and Pack 5 combat gold caps (`14/12/10 → 6g`).
- **Remaining v0.23 watch item:** Pack 5 Economy/Greed still reaches **80% full-run WR** (`164g` avg gold vs. `136g` competent). In v0.23, consider tuning `Bag of Holding` + Pack 5 gold scaling slightly if targeting `65–70%` optimal WR.

### Mistake 5 — Boss Disparity (War Band Too Weak)
- **What happened:** Pre-balance **War Band (`orcs`, `m: 0.81`)** was a free win (`100%` WR at Act 2 T3 and Act 3 T4 vs. `60–70%` for Owlbear and Brood Mother).
- **Fix applied:** Raised `orcs` `m: 0.81 → 0.96`, `owlbear` `0.80 → 0.87`, `spider` `0.73 → 0.78`. Full-run boss deaths now spread evenly across **Brood Mother (21)**, **Owlbear (21)**, and **War Band (12)**.

### Mistake 6 — Item Rarity-Per-Gold Inversion & Gold-Trap Items
- **What happened:** Common (`2g`) and Uncommon (`3g`) items delivered **2×–4× more score per gold** than Very Rare (`7g`) items. Meanwhile, `Clockwork Amulet`, `Potion of Healing`, `Periapt of Wound Closure`, and `Rod of the Pact Keeper` tested near zero lift.
- **Open v0.23 opportunity:** Re-price or buff the bottom-tier utility items (especially `Clockwork Amulet`, `Potion of Healing`, `Periapt of Wound Closure`, `Rod of the Pact Keeper`) and consider `ECON.itemPrice: [2, 3, 4, 6]` so late-game Rares/Very Rares feel as rewarding per gold as early Uncommons.

## 3. Summary of Applied v0.22 Balance Pass

### Global Mechanics, Enemy Curve & Economy
- **Combat & Keywords ([`src/05-engine.js`](../src/05-engine.js)):** Damage-role `+12%` `u.dm`; Hunter’s Mark `+22%` party damage + `1d10+WIS` Ranger proc; Bardic Inspiration **Inspired Flourish** (`+25%` dmg & `+25%` Haste for `4.5s * chaK`); passive Sneak Attack `+1d6`; Fire Burn feed `45% → 55%`; **28s Overtime** (`OT_STEP = 5s`, `−15%` Heal/Ward per stack, max `−60%`).
- **Enemy Scaling & Economy ([`src/06-adventure.js`](../src/06-adventure.js)):** `CURVE.share: [.45,.60,.76,.88,1]`, `CURVE.grow: 1.225`, `CURVE.dmgGrow: 1.28`, `CURVE.ease: [0.95,1.14,1.28,1.40,1.46]`, `d===1: .96/.95`, `ADV.bossDepth: [8.2,13.0,17.5]`, `ADV.eliteDepth: 1.3`, `ADV.eliteK: 1.26`, `THREATS` boss parity (`orcs .81→.96`, `owlbear .80→.87`, `spider .73→.78`), `ECON.winGold: 5+floor(d/5)`, `ECON.heroPrice: [0,3,6,9,13]`, `ADV.eliteGold: 3`, `restAt: +4g`.

### Individual Hero Adjustments ([`src/03-heroes.js`](../src/03-heroes.js), [`src/05-engine.js`](../src/05-engine.js))
- **Nerfed / Fixed:** **Zoe** (`u.dm²` fix, Rummage heal/Ward trimmed, Runic Upgrade capped at 2×`+8%`, Just the Thing 1s CD), **Faelan** (Ward `[12,18,24,28]+2×INT` cap `55%`, High refund `35→18`, Projected Ward `1.35→1.18`, Arcane Recovery `20→12`, duplicate `stats.ward` fixed), **Mother Laurel** (`u.dm²` fix, Bloom trimmed, Bountiful Vines 1s CD & T4 2 targets), **Juniper** (Circle of the Land 1s CD, T4 2 targets, Ward `0.45×`), **Kaska** (`u.dm²` fix, Panther HP `0.32/0.44`, Pack Hunt `7 HP + 8 Charge`, 1.2s CD), **Gilthrax** (Ward/item scaling trimmed, cap `65%`, reflect `3s`, gold cap `6g`), **Brother Jamison** (`u.dm²` fix, Balm of Peace trimmed), **Pillar** (`u.dm²` fix), **Toot** & **Gains** (gold cap `6g`), **Wallaby** (gold cap `8g`).
- **Buffed:** **Rosalind** (`102` HP, Mockery `1d8+2` / `−22%` dmg + Stagger, `+15/18` Charge + Inspired Flourish), **Cinder** (Fireball `[3,5,6,8]d6`, Burn `[5,8,12,16]`, Twinned `5d6`, detonation `2.2×`, Wildfire on every cast), **Nettle** (`1d8/1d10` Hex + Pact Boon `+16` Charge & heal), **Zark** (`6%` DR/Rage stack + `10%/15%` heal on High/20), **Naill** (Ki Strike `2d10+2×WIS` + Alert), **Wallaby** (`106` HP, Backstab `[3,5,7,9]d6`, shadow evade on kill), **Arthur** (`+3` flat/Volley arrow), **Lorcan** (`104` HP, Longbow flat `3` + Hunter’s Mark rework), **Bram** (`146` HP), **Saint** (`108` HP, faster Bell, Necrosis `[4,10,16,22]` + `u.dm²` fix), **Lumen** (`u.dm²` fix + Damage `+12%`), **Velith** & **Corvin** (Damage `+12%` + Sneak Attack / Hunter’s Mark buffs).

## 5. Pre-Balance Items vs. Tiers & Gold Efficiency Reference

- **Items beat Tier-ups gold-for-gold in midgame:** At Tier II (`d=11.5`), 4 Common items (`8g`) lift WR from `18.8% → 62.5%`, and 8 mixed items (`~28g`) hit `100%` WR — beating upgrading all 4 heroes to Tier III (`~32g`, `93.8%` WR).
- **Item-to-Hero Fit Matters (`+37.5pp` WR):** 4 Matched Rares (`20g`) score `93.8%` WR vs. `56.3%` for 4 Mismatched Rares.
- **Highest Value-per-Gold Staples:** `Bracers of Archery` (3g), `Pearl of Power` (3g), `+1 Longsword` (2g), `Wand of the War Mage` (2g), `Leather Armor` (2g), `Bloodwell Vial` (3g), `Cloak of Elvenkind` (3g), `Moon Sickle` (3g), `Bag of Holding` (3g — pays for itself in 3 wins).
- **Live Synergy Standouts:** `Staff of Power` (`+32.8–50.2` on Faelan/Zoe), `Ring of Spell Storing` (`+29.8–46.7` on Lorcan/Faelan/Zoe), `Staff of the Woodlands` (`+44.2–46.1` on Kaska/Faelan), `Staff of Healing` (`+48.1` on Kaska), `Ring of Regeneration` (`+27.7–34.7` on Kaska/Gilthrax/Bram), `Sun Blade` (`+20.0–24.1` on Sir Dane/Arthur/Lorcan), `Oathbow` (`+25.8` on Lorcan), `Dagger of Venom` (`+9.3–23.4` on Velith/Corvin).

## 6. Iteration Checklist for v0.23+

1. **Keep automated meta checks in CI/dev workflow:** Re-run headless 3-Act bot personas (`Casual`, `Zero-Item`, `Competent`, `Synergy`, `Greed`) after every hero/item/curve patch so difficulty never silently regresses back to 90–100% auto-wins.
2. **Audit remaining C-tier solo specialists (`Lorcan`, `Rosalind`, `Gains`, `Lumen`, `Wallaby`, `Saint`, `Toot`):** Decide intentionally which ones are *meant* to be narrow synergy/economy specialists (e.g., `Toot`, `Gains`, `Lumen`) vs. which need slightly higher baseline floors (`Wallaby`, `Saint`, `Lorcan` without Turn-1 Charge).
3. **Watch the top two Act 3 comps (`Wild Shape Moon & Land` 100% WR, `Arcane Recovery Battery` 91.7% WR):** Both still clear Act 3 Tier III at `d=13.8` comfortably — good aspirational payoffs, but keep an eye on Juniper+Laurel+Kaska stacking if adding new Wild Shape items or heroes.
4. **Upgrade low-ROI items:** Buff `Clockwork Amulet`, `Potion of Healing`, `Periapt of Wound Closure`, and `Rod of the Pact Keeper`, and test `ECON.itemPrice: [2, 3, 4, 6]` so Very Rare items compete cleanly on gold efficiency.
5. **Rebuilding the Wiki Stat Tracker:** After future simulation sweeps, update the JSON snapshots in [`data/v0.22-meta/`](../data/v0.22-meta/) and run `node tools/build-wiki-data.mjs` to refresh [`data/wiki-meta-stats.js`](../data/wiki-meta-stats.js) for [`tools/wiki.html`](../tools/wiki.html).
