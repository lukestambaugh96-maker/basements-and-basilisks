# Basements & Basilisks v0.23 — Character & System Design Philosophy

**Status:** Standing Design Bible (v0.23+)  
**Scope:** Governs all character kits, class keywords, card UI, progression layers, and future packs unless explicitly overridden by the designer.

> **North Star:** *Bloat is the enemy; clarity is the goal.* A player recruiting a Tier I hero in the shop should understand what that hero does on the board in **under 5 seconds**.

---

## 1. The Five Mechanical Pillars of Every Hero

Every playable hero is built from exactly five structured pillars — nothing extra stapled onto the base card:

1. **Signature Verb:** One unmistakable board identity (e.g., *Dodge & Counter*, *Cleave, Root & Drain*, *Curse & Detonate*). If you cannot describe what a hero does in 2–4 words, the kit is unfocused.
2. **1 Ability:** One ability forever (unless altered by an opt-in Quest). Clean numeric scaling across Low / Mid / High / Nat 20. Avoid piling 3–4 unrelated riders onto High or Nat 20 rolls.
3. **1 Passive *(Optional, Compensator Only)*:** Omitted by default. Only include a passive when a kit genuinely needs a structural compensator to function (e.g., *Arthur’s Alert* for melee evasion, *Wallaby’s Lucky* to prevent backline leap fizzles, *Zark’s Relentless Endurance* to survive long enough to stack Rage, *Jamison’s Gentle Strike* to make weak melee autos heal).
   - **Never** add "included in stats" flavor passives (*Human Versatility*, *Fleet of Foot*, *Warden’s Reach*, *Gnome Cunning*).
   - **Never** add niche immunities that rarely fire (*Stout*, *Fey Ancestry*, *Brave*, *Hellish Resistance*).
   - **Never** add a passive that merely repeats what the hero’s Ability or Class Keyword already says (*Pack Rat*, *Hoard Scales*, *Transmuter’s Eye*, *Predator’s Leap*).
4. **Class Keyword Interaction:** One crisp class-defining hook (two short ones only for Paladins, who always pair **Aura + Smite**).
   - **Zero Trigger Duplication:** Never state the same trigger in both the Class Keyword box and the Ability’s High/Nat 20 lines. Either the Class Keyword has its own independent trigger (e.g., Arthur evading, Gilthrax’s Ward breaking, Zoe rescuing an ally below 50% HP), or it defines the effect concisely without repeating the Ability text.
   - **Zero Fake Aliases or Duplicate Keywords:** Never invent a second name for an existing mechanic (e.g., *"Sear"* is just **Burn**; *"Afterimage"* is just **Evasion**).
5. **2 Simple Specializations *(Tier II — renamed from "Paths")*:**
   - Strictly **one line of text maximum** per Specialization (e.g., *"X now does Y"*, *"Kills make Arthur Alert"*, *"Basic attacks heal the lowest-HP ally for 25% of damage dealt"*).
   - No multi-clause paragraphs, no separate Tier IV riders attached to Specializations.
   - Typically offer one **Vertical** upgrade (deepens the hero’s own loop) and one **Pivot / Team** upgrade (wires the hero into teammates or an alternate playstyle).

## 2. Strict Progressive Disclosure on Hero Cards

Card readability is enforced structurally by **never showing choices the player has not made yet**:

- **Tier I (Starter Selection & Shop):** Displays **only** the hero’s base kit — Stats/Attributes, Class Keyword(s), optional Compensator Passive (if any), and 1 Ability.
  - **No** Tier II Specializations shown on Tier I cards.
  - **No** Tier IV preview shown on Tier I–III cards.
  - **No** Hero Quest or Multiclass box shown unless the player opted into them at their map nodes.
- **Tier II (Specialization Picker):** When a hero reaches Tier II (via Upgrade or pre-tiered Act 2–3 recruitment), the player chooses 1 of 2 **Specializations**. Only the **chosen 1-line Specialization** is appended to the card. *(Campfires still allow retraining.)*
- **Tier IV (Epic Boon Picker):** When a hero reaches Tier IV, the player chooses 1 of 3 universal **Epic Boons** drawn randomly from a shared deck. Only the **chosen 1-line Epic Boon** is appended to the card.
- **Quest Board (`❖`) & Multiclass Node:** Only heroes whose Quest or Multiclass dip you explicitly picked up display those lines on their card.

## 3. Tier IV Progression: Universal Epic Boons

Per-hero Tier IV rules (`48` bespoke clauses across 24 heroes) are replaced by a shared **Epic Boon Deck** inspired by high-level D&D:

- **Trigger:** Hitting Tier IV on any hero deals **3 random Epic Boons** from the shared deck; pick **1**.
- **Design Goal:** Powerful, 1-line, universal upgrades that either **supercharge what the hero already does** or **change how the player thinks about using that hero** in Act 3.
- **Starter Reference Pool (Examples):**
  - *Boon of Irresistible Offense:* All damage dealt ignores AC and SR.
  - *Boon of Spell Recall:* Abilities cast a second time on High and Nat 20.
  - *Boon of Recovery:* First time dropping to 0 HP each fight, revive at 50% HP *(triggers **after** Cleric Multiclass Channel Divinity so a hero can benefit from both)*.
  - *Boon of Combat Prowess:* Basic attacks heal you for 30% of damage dealt.
  - *Boon of Fate:* Natural 19s count as Nat 20s.
  - *Boon of Speed:* +35% Attack Speed and +25% Charge Speed.
  - *Boon of Fortitude:* +40% Max HP and start every fight with a Ward equal to 25% Max HP.
  - *Boon of the Night Spirit:* +25% Evasion; evading an attack grants +15 Charge.
  - *Boon of Dimensional Travel:* Starts combat with +50 Charge and teleports to safety at 40% HP.

## 4. Optional Hero Quests (Quest Board `❖` Node)

Hero Quests are **no longer innate** to hero cards:

- **Opt-In at Quest Board (`❖`):** Visiting a Quest Board node lets you pick **1 hero in your party** to accept their personal Quest *(or skip / take a small Gold bounty)*.
- **Why Opt-In:** Because Quests can drastically alter a hero (e.g., turning Arthur’s ranged *Longbow Volley* into a melee Fire cone, turning Sir Dane or Pillar into a 2×2 Large unit), players should choose them deliberately as build pivots — never get forced into them mid-run.
- **Format:** **1 short goal** (tuned for mid-run completion) → **1 bold, single-line transformative reward** (preserving all custom Quest/Form art assets).

---

## 5. Multiclassing Map Node

A dedicated map node lets a hero take a **Multiclass Dip** into a second D&D class:

- **Rules:**
  - Each hero can hold **at most 1 Multiclass dip** and cannot pick their own base class.
  - Visiting a Multiclass node lets you pick a hero and choose from **3 random other classes**.
  - Grants **both** the **Class Keyword** (dynamically added to `u.kw`, unlocking that class’s **Favoured items** and **party synergies**) and **1 universal, single-line passive** applicable to any hero.
  - Strictly progressive-disclosed: zero Multiclass UI appears on a hero card until they actually multiclass.
- **Lethal-Damage Trigger Priority Order:**
  1. **Relentless Endurance** *(Zark’s survive-at-1-HP passive)*
  2. **Cleric Multiclass (*Channel Divinity* revive at 25% HP)** — triggers *before* Tier IV Epic Boons *(and can be reset by Cleric Channel Divinity resets like Bram’s Nat 20)*
  3. **Boon of Recovery** *(Tier IV Epic Boon — revive at 50% HP)*
- **12-Class Universal Multiclass Dip Pool:**

| Class Dip | Keyword Granted | Universal 1-Line Passive |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fighter** | *Action Surge* | Make an extra basic attack immediately after casting your ability. |
| **Rogue** | *Sneak Attack* | Basic attacks deal **+1d6** bonus damage against flanked or Exposed foes *(1/s)*. |
| **Cleric** | *Channel Divinity* | Once per fight, when dropping to 0 HP, revive at **25% Max HP**. |
| **Paladin** | *Smite* | Every 3rd basic attack deals **+1d6 Radiant** damage. |
| **Wizard** | *Arcane Recovery* | **High** and **Nat 20** casts recover **+25 Charge**. |
| **Barbarian** | *Rage* | Dropping below 50% HP grants **Rage** *(+15% damage reduction & +20% Attack Speed)*. |
| **Bard** | *Bardic Inspiration* | First cast each fight grants the closest ally a **d6 Inspiration Die**. |
| **Druid** | *Wild Shape* | Starts every fight with a **Primal Ward** *(20% Max HP)* that reflects **Thorns**. |
| **Ranger** | *Hunter’s Mark* | Every 3rd basic attack applies a stack of **Hunter’s Mark** *(+1d4 damage per hit)*. |
| **Monk** | *Ki* | Every 4th basic attack deals **+2d6** damage and **Staggers** the target. |
| **Sorcerer** | *Metamagic* | Ability damage and healing are increased by **+20%**. |
| **Warlock** | *Hex* | First attack applies **Hex** *(+1d4 necrotic on hits; jumps to another foe on death)*. |

## 6. Class Consistency & Targeting Rules

To keep the 12-class roster intuitive as new packs are added:

- **Paladins (`Aura` + `Smite`):** Every Paladin carries **both** an **Aura** and a **Smite**, balanced against each other (e.g., Sir Dane has a heavy Radiant single-target Smite + light defensive Aura; Mother Laurel has a strong frontline Drain Aura + lighter cleave-friendly Nature Smite).
- **Warlocks (Unique Patron `Hex`):** **Hex** is a status that lives on the **enemy**, and every Warlock brings their own Patron’s unique Hex (e.g., Nettle’s *Infernal Hex* explodes in Hellfire and jumps on death; Lumen’s *Radiant Hex* stacks on the enemy and grants Lumen Attack Speed per stack). Avoid vague secondary jargon like "Pact Boon".
- **Rangers (`Hunter’s Mark`):** Standardized across all Rangers — **Marked foes take +1d4 bonus damage from every hit (stacks)**, creating instant clarity on bosses and natural synergy with rapid/multi-hit allies.
- **Bards (Interactable Spatial Targeting):** Bard buffs (*Rosalind’s Lore Die*, *Toot’s Mote of Potential*) and link supports (*Jamison’s Emboldening Bond*) always target the **closest eligible ally** — never an opaque hidden formula — and highlight their linked target during board deployment so positioning is an intentional player decision.
- **Wizards (Two Distinct Cadence Archetypes):** Wizards do **not** all have to be rapid spell-spammers. Some are **rapid-casters** (*Faelan*) who loop quick Wards; others are **heavy-cooldown, high-impact casters** (*Gains*) whose long cast times shrink dramatically with INT.
- **Basic Attacks Stay Simple Unless They’re the Point:** If a hero is ability-driven (*Faelan, Juniper, Cinder, Nettle*), their basic attack is plain damage + Charge with zero hidden riders. Only heroes whose basic attack is core to their identity (*Mother Laurel’s Cleave, Lumen’s Radiant Beam, Rosalind’s Vicious Mockery, Jamison’s Gentle Strike, Toot’s 0-dmg Trumpet, Gains’s Gold-chance Bolt, Corvin’s Twin Blades*) carry a basic-attack behavior.

## 7. Base Stats, Scaling & Economy Guardrails

- **Base Stats Anchor the Hero’s Role, Not Free Carry Scaling:** A Tank like Mother Laurel has Tank-level baseline damage at 0 bonus stats; turning her into a damage carry should come from intentional player itemization and ASIs, not overtuned base numbers.
- **Multiplicative Primary Scaling (Dota 2 Principle):** Every ability score provides a clear universal benefit to any hero, while scaling **multiplicatively** on heroes whose kit natively uses that score (e.g., DEX gives Damage + Attack Speed + AC to DEX carries; INT gives Cast Frequency + Spell Power to Wizards).
- **Radiant Damage Is Rare:** Formerly "Holy Damage", **Radiant Damage** ignores AC and SR and stops undead from rising. Keep sources scarce and deliberate (Sir Dane’s heavy Smite, Lumen’s lower-die `d6` beams, select divine specializations) so AC and SR remain meaningful.
- **Organic Gold Generation Over Clunky Caps:** Prefer probabilistic Gold triggers on weak/slow actions (*Toot’s 0-dmg trumpet*, *Gains’s slow wizard bolt*) and kill bounties (*Toot’s Motes*, *Gilded kills*) over slapping arbitrary hard combat caps across every hero in a pack.
- **Technical Engine Guardrails (Carried from v0.22 Meta Lessons):**
  1. Never pre-multiply heals by `u.dm` at call sites unless `{ raw: 1 }` is passed (prevent `u.dm²` double-scaling).
  2. Every recursive combat buff (`dm`, `DR`, `Haste`, `Ward`, `Evasion`) must have a clear cap or multiplicative diminishing curve.
  3. Every cross-hero reactive trigger must carry an explicit `≥ 1.0s` internal cooldown matching `MIN_CAST_INTERVAL = 1.0`.
