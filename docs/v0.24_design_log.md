## 1. Problem Statement (Why v0.24 Happened)

Coming out of v0.23 (24 reworked heroes, 13 Class Keywords, 82 items, Cubed AC/SR `K=16`, 3-Act DAG), the core loop still did not feel satisfying to play:

- **Output randomness muddied input intentionality:** Because live d20 combat variance decides close fights, players could not easily tell whether they won or lost because of their decisions or because of the dice — making choices feel arbitrary.
- **Lack of moment-to-moment direction & knowledge gap:** Players needed clearer signals for what they should be doing each turn (Map → Shop → Deployment) without suffering from an opaque knowledge gap.
- **Flat, incremental shop decisions:** Saving gold or buying a lower-tier hero did not feel worthwhile when every shop offer was just a small flat power bump.
- **Flat synergies:** Existing synergies felt like they added flat stats rather than opening new build directions.

### Non-Negotiable Constraints Set by the Owner
1. **Combat stays 100% hands-off.** No active mid-combat inputs.
2. **Keep live d20 / output randomness as-is.** The hook of teambuilding is compensating for inconsistency.
3. **Preserve all 24 reworked heroes**, their balance, 13 Class Keywords, 48 Specializations, 9 Epic Boons, 12 Multiclass dips, and 24 Opt-In Quests.
4. **No TFT-style trait-count set bonuses** on heroes — explore team-wide keyword modifiers instead to encourage Multiclassing.

## 2. Design Decisions Made & Why

All four pillars below were implemented together in one pass ("Let's try all 4 at once") so their interactions could be playtested as a complete loop:

### Pillar 1 — Soft Lanes ("Breach & Flank") & 13 Redesigned Maps
- **Decision:** Split all 13 battle maps into 2-lane (`10×10`) or 3-lane (`11×10`) arenas with terrain dividers across rows `2..6` and open crossover bridges on rows `0..1` and `8..9`, rather than Hard (sealed) lanes.
- **Why:** To make deployment placement a readable puzzle where each lane has distinct enemies and explicit win/loss stakes (`Main Breach`, `Dark Ritual 12s`, `Supply Cart 14s`, `Duel Choke · 1 Hero Max`, `Battle Shrine`, `Boss Throne`), while still allowing lane winners to cross over and flank ("Breach & Flank") so fights stay dynamic and `2×2` Large units still path cleanly.

### Pillar 2 — Challenge Rating (`Party CR` vs. `Encounter CR`) & Post-Combat Autopsy
- **Decision:** Add deterministic `Party CR` and pre-scouted `Encounter CR` on map nodes and deployment lane headers (`Favoured` / `Even` / `Dangerous` / `Deadly`), plus a Post-Combat Autopsy breakdown (Lane outcomes, House Rule contributions, AC/SR mitigation warnings, and a d20 Luck Audit comparing actual average roll against `10.5`).
- **Why:** To eliminate blind betting on encounter difficulty and separate build/mitigation failures from d20 luck after every fight — directly addressing the knowledge gap and input-intentionality problem.

### Pillar 3 — Shop-Offered & Boss-Drafted Party Modifiers → **House Rules** (6-Slot Cap) + Exponential Curve
- **Decision:** Introduce 36 permanent party-wide modifiers offered in both the Camp Shop and 1-of-3 Boss/Miniboss drafts, inspired by *Guildrun* relics and paired with a *Balatro*-style piecewise exponential 3-Act difficulty curve.
- **Naming Decision:** Rejected "Keyword Resonances" and "Guild Crests" in favor of **House Rules** — playful tabletop/basement flavor matching *Basements & Basilisks*.
- **Capacity Decision:** Capped at **`HOUSE_RULE_MAX = 6`** slots (with 50% gold sell/replace flow) instead of unlimited, specifically to test whether a slot cap creates meaningful late-game pivot/optimization tension or feels too restrictive.
- **Why:** To encourage multiplicative teambuilding and give **Multiclass (`✧`)** shrines a clear payoff via 13 Class-Keyword House Rules, without adding TFT-style trait counters onto hero cards.

### Pillar 4 — Immediate Power vs. Future Power Shop Economy
- **Decision:** Add three future-power levers to the Camp Shop alongside immediate flat upgrades:
  1. **Camp Rank (`I → IV`):** Costs `7g / 11g / 15g` (discounted `-1g` per combat win); raises Gold Interest cap (`+5g → +7g → +9g → +12g`), unlocks Rare/Legendary House Rules and a 3rd House Rule slot, boosts higher-tier hero/item odds, and grants 1 free reroll per visit at Rank IV.
  2. **Raised Gold Interest:** `1g` per `5g` held, gated by Camp Rank (plus `+4g` cap via *Guild Treasury*).
  3. **6 `🌱 Scaling` Uncommon Items:** 1 per attribute (`STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA`, preserving exact 6-attribute parity → 88 total items) that start weak for `4g` and gain `+1` permanent stack on every combat victory while equipped.
- **Why:** So the shop implicitly asks *"do I need power right now to beat the next CR check, or can I invest for Acts 2–3?"*

## 4. Verification, Calibration & Bugs Fixed During v0.24

### Headless Calibration (`node tools/verify-v024.mjs 25`, 125 campaigns / 1,137 fights)
- **Invariants:** All passed (24 heroes, 13 Class Keywords, 9 Epic Boons, 12 Multiclass dips, 36 House Rules, 88 items with exact 6-attribute parity, Cubed AC/SR `K=16`, all 13 maps & 35 encounters).
- **CR Verdict Correlation:** `Favoured 97.6%` (925 fights), `Even 69.0%` (129), `Dangerous 39.7%` (58), `Deadly 4.0%` (25).
- **5-Persona 3-Act Win Rates (25 runs/persona):** `flat_only 4.0%`, `greed 40.0%`, `naive 44.0%`, `engine 52.0%`, `competent 68.0%`.
  *(Note: Subsequent 60-run ablation in `analysis/ablate-60.json` / `PROJECT_HANDOFF_v0.24.md` §6 confirmed `naive 45%`, `competent 63%`, `engine 62%`, `greed 38%` and showed that Camp Rank and Scaling items were net-negative for bots while the `naive` floor remained too high.)*

### Bugs Caught & Fixed During v0.24 Implementation
1. **Clutter tile overlap on `shadow_crypt` & `tresendar_cellar`:** Initial lane redesign placed enemy spawns on blocked clutter tiles (`c`); fixed coordinates so all 35 encounters passed `passable()` and duplicate-spawn checks.
2. **`BR.bossCR` typo in `src/11-shell.js`:** `bossRead()` returned `cr`, not `bossCR`; fixed so Boss CR rendered cleanly on the Map sidebar.
3. **Lane Header overlay overlap in combat:** Fixed via `#boardWrap { padding-top: 54px }`, narrower `.c-ov-tl`, and `-68` height offset in `resize()`.
4. **Wiki `itemRows` E2E assertion:** `tools/wiki.html` read pre-computed `data/wiki-meta-stats.js` (82 items prior to `2bfbdc5`), fixed assertion to `>= 82`.
5. **Browser E2E:** `node tools/test-e2e-chromium.mjs` passed all v0.24 assertions with 0 console/runtime errors.

## 5. Post-Playtest Critique & Lessons Learned (Closing v0.24 → v0.25)

After pushing and playtesting v0.24, the owner provided direct playtest feedback that closed out the v0.24 experiment and directly informed the v0.25 redesign ([`v0.25_design_direction.md`](v0.25_design_direction.md)):

1. **Soft Lanes felt awkward in actual play:**
   > *"Remove the lanes for now. The idea is sound but playing it feels very awkward. Fights do need to feel more intentionally designed for positioning though, I am working out how to do that."*
2. **House Rules are promising, but raw multiplier copy & flat bonuses miss the mark:**
   > *"House rules are a good addition, but the bonuses they offer read terribly. ... House rules should influence which heroes the player chooses to field and what multiclasses they choose to take and not like straight bonuses."*
   - Clunky formula copy (`×1.07`, `×1.12`, `×1.28`) read poorly at a glance, and straight stat/damage multipliers acted like generic stat sticks instead of build-around pulls that change hero drafting and Multiclass choices.
3. **Scaling Items scaled too quickly in play:**
   > *"Scaling items scale way too quickly."*
   - Uncapped `+1` stack on every combat victory rapidly snowballed 4g Uncommon items in human play (even while bot ablation showed bots mis-evaluated them against raw higher-tier items).
4. **Missing Combat Telemetry Baseline:**
   > *"I need stats on how many attacks, casts and wards are generated in a fight and so on."*
   - Future House Rule thresholds and item/hero scaling need concrete per-fight distributions (basic attacks, ability casts, wards, heals, keyword procs) rather than guesswork.

*(No v0.24 post-playtest code changes were pushed from this chat because the project pivoted to v0.25 in [`v0.25_design_direction.md`](v0.25_design_direction.md).)*
