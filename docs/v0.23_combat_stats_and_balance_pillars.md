# Basements & Basilisks v0.23 — Combat Stats, Mitigation & Balance Pillars

**Status:** Core Combat Math & Balancing Reference (Heroes, Enemies & Bosses)  
**Purpose:** Defines every derived combat stat, the 6 ability scores, the cubed AC/SR mitigation curve, the 4-axis defense model, and enemy/boss balancing rules so all units are tuned against one consistent math foundation.

---

## 1. Derived Combat Stats Held by Units

Every unit (hero, enemy, or boss) has **unique base stats** tailored to its role and Signature Verb. Ability scores, items, and Tiers act as **bonuses** layered on top of those base stats.

### Defense Stats
| Stat | Base & Scaling | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Max HP** | `(baseHp + 18 × CON + itemHp) × 1.45^(Tier−1)` | Primary buffer against all damage, especially Radiant & DoTs. |
| **AC** *(Armor Class)* | `baseAC + DEX + itemAC + buffs − debuffs` | Mitigates **Physical** damage via the Cubed Curve (§3). |
| **SR** *(Spell Resistance)* | `baseSR + WIS + itemSR + buffs − debuffs` | Mitigates **Magic** damage via the Cubed Curve (§3). |
| **Evasion** *(New, Rare)* | `0%` base *(never innate in base stats)* | `% chance` to take **0 damage from enemy basic attacks** *(spells/abilities still hit)*. Stacks **multiplicatively**: `1 − (1 − a)(1 − b)`. |
| **HP Regen** | `(0.5 + 0.5 × max(0, CON) + itemRegen) × 1.45^(Tier−1)` | Passive HP/sec; natural counter to whittling DoTs (Burn & Poison). |
| **Poison Resist** | `10% × max(0, CON)` *(cap 70%)* | Slows Poison tick accumulation. |
| **CC Recovery** | `+10% × max(0, CHA)` faster decay | Reduces effective Stun and Root durations. |
| **Ward** | Temporary shield *(capped by source)* | Absorbs damage and Necrosis before HP. |

### Offense Stats
| Stat | Base & Scaling | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weapon Dice & Hits** | `NdS + flat`, `hits` count, damage kind | Base per-hit profile; slow heavy weapons love Attack Speed, fast multi-hit weapons love flat damage / on-hit. |
| **Attack Damage Bonus** | `weaponAttr mod + itemDmg` | Flat damage added to basic attacks *(v0.23 fix: applies to Radiant basic attacks like Lumen’s Sacred Beam too)*. |
| **Attack Interval (`iv` / BAT)** | `baseIv × COMBAT_TEMPO / (1 + haste / 100)` | Lower = faster attacks. `baseIv` acts like Dota 2’s **Base Attack Time (BAT)**. |
| **Armor Pen (`pen`)** | `max(0, STR)` *(melee/thrown)* `+ itemPen` | Reduces target’s effective AC before the Cubed Curve (floor 6–10). |
| **Spell Pen (`spen`)** | `itemSpen` | Reduces target’s effective SR before the Cubed Curve. |
| **Tier Multiplier (`dm`)** | `1.30^(Tier−1)` | Scales damage and healing across Tiers I–IV *(avoid hidden role-based `dm` hacks; tune base stats directly)*. |
| **On-Hit Riders** | Kit + item on-hit list | Triggered by basic attacks and abilities tagged `on-hit`. |

### Casting & d20 Stats
| Stat | Base & Scaling | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Charge Rate (`cr`, `pa`, `chgX`)** | Base passive/trigger Charge × `(1 + INT% + itemCharge%)` | Global cap: **`MIN_CAST_INTERVAL = 1.0s`** (max 1 Charge-cast/sec). |
| **Charge Trigger (`trig`)** | Role-based: Tank = `Struck`, Damage = `Striking`, Support = `Watchful` | Governs how each role generates active combat Charge. |
| **Ability d20 Bonus (`rollBonus`)** | `keyAttr mod + itemRollBonus + temp` | Added to ability d20 roll. |
| **High-Roll Shift (`hiShift`)** | `floor(max(0, keyAttr mod) / 2)` | Lowers the threshold required to hit the **High** band. |
| **Min d20 Floor (`d20Floor`)** | `min(10, 1 + max(0, WIS))` | Minimum natural d20 result *(at `WIS ≥ +1`, cannot roll Nat 1 Fizzle)*. |
| **Ability Flat Bonus (`bon.k`)** | `keyAttr mod` | Flat bonus added to ability damage and healing. |
| **Buff/Debuff Potency (`chaPotency`)** | `1 + 8% × max(0, CHA)` | Scales duration/potency of buffs, debuffs, statuses, and Wards. |

### Board & Spatial Stats
| Stat | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Range (`rng`)** | Attack reach in hexes (`1` = true frontline melee; `4–5` = ranged). |
| **Move Speed (`ft`)** | Hex traversal speed (`25–40 ft`). |
| **Size (`sz`)** | `1×1` Medium or `2×2` Large *(Quest forms: mounted Sir Dane, Colossus Pillar; large bosses)*. |
| **Intercept Chargers** | Melee heroes with `STR ≥ +3` automatically intercept leaping enemy Chargers. |

## 2. The Six Ability Scores (Dota-Style Multiplicative Scaling)

Every ability score helps **any** hero universally, while scaling **multiplicatively** for heroes whose Dual-Attribute Pair (`[weaponAttr, keyAttr]`) natively uses that score:

| Score | Universal Effect (Every Hero) | Multiplicative Payoff on Native Heroes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **STR** | `+1 Armor Pen` *(melee/thrown)* · Intercept Chargers at `STR ≥ +3` | Adds melee weapon damage alongside Armor Pen (*Arthur, Dane, Zark, Pillar, Mother Laurel*). |
| **DEX** | `+4% Attack Speed` · `+1 AC` | Grants **`+6%` Attack Speed + Weapon Damage + AC** simultaneously (*Wallaby, Lorcan, Naill, Velith, Corvin*). |
| **CON** | `+18 Max HP (×Tier)` · `+0.5 HP Regen/s` · `+10% Poison Resist` | Also scales ability/High rolls on CON tanks (*Zark, Gilthrax*). |
| **INT** | `+10% Charge Speed` *(reduces cast time)* | Cuts cast cooldowns **and** boosts spell power/d20 on Wizards — rewarding both **rapid-casters** (*Faelan*) and **heavy-cooldown nukers** (*Gains*). |
| **WIS** | `+1 SR` · `+1 Min Natural d20 Floor` *(cap 10)* | Stacks reliability + SR + ability power on WIS casters (*Bram, Juniper, Mother Laurel*). |
| **CHA** | `+8% Buff/Debuff/Status/Ward Potency & Duration` · `+10% CC Recovery` | Multiplies buffs, Bonds, Inspiration, Burn, Hexes, and Necrosis (*Rosalind, Cinder, Nettle, Lumen, Jamison, Toot*). |

## 3. The Cubed AC / SR Mitigation Curve

Replaces v0.22’s `10 / AC` formula (where `AC 10` gave `0%` mitigation and `AC 8` amplified damage).

$$\text{DR}(x) = \frac{x^3}{x^3 + K^3} \qquad \text{Damage Taken} = \text{Raw} \times \frac{K^3}{x^3 + K^3} \qquad \text{EHP Multiplier} = 1 + \left(\frac{x}{K}\right)^3$$

Recommended midpoint **`K = 14`** *(keeps `AC 10` at a middling standard 27% DR and `AC 20` at a fortress-like 75% DR without turning `AC 24` into an unkillable `9×` cliff)*:

| AC / SR | **Cube(`K = 14`) DR** | **Cube(`K = 14`) EHP** | Benchmark Role / Tier |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **8** | 15.7% | `1.19×` | Unarmored / Exposed squishy or minion |
| **10** | **26.7%** | `1.36×` | Standard baseline *(unimpressive, normal mitigation)* |
| **12** | 38.6% | `1.63×` | Light armor / agile backliner |
| **14** | **50.0%** | `2.00×` | Solid medium armor / bruiser |
| **16** | 59.9% | `2.49×` | Frontline Tank baseline |
| **18** | 68.0% | `3.13×` | Armored Tank / Shielded knight |
| **20** | **74.5%** | `3.92×` | Heavy Plate fortress *(D&D bounded-accuracy peak)* |
| **22** | 79.5% | `4.88×` | Heavily itemized Tank |
| **24** | 83.4% | `6.04×` | Extreme cap *(requires Armor Pen, Exposed, Magic, or Radiant)* |

**Design Implications:**
- **Bounded Accuracy Matters:** Keep normal hero/enemy AC & SR inside the **`10–20`** band so every `+1` or `−2` (Exposed, STR Armor Pen, Plate Armor) creates a massive, felt shift on the board.
- **High-AC Counterplay:** Dropping a `20 AC` boss to `16 AC` via **STR Armor Pen** + **Exposed** drops their physical EHP from `3.92×` down to `2.49×` — a huge, visible tactical payoff!

## 4. The Four-Axis Damage & Defense Model

Every damage type has one clear defensive counter and one clear offensive counter-tool:

| Damage Type | Mitigated By | Bypassed / Punished By | Design Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical** *(Slashing, Piercing, Bludgeoning)* | **AC** + **Evasion** *(basic attacks only)* | **STR Armor Pen**, **Exposed (−2 AC)** | Backbone of melee/ranged fighters, archers, rogues, and beasts. |
| **Magic** *(Fire, Cold, Nature, Necrotic, Psychic, Force, Thunder)* | **SR** *(abilities ignore Evasion)* | **Spell Pen**, **Exposed (−2 SR)** | Includes Mother Laurel’s *Verdant Smite* (Nature), fireballs, bolts, bells. |
| **DoTs** *(Burn, Poison)* | **CON** *(Max HP, HP Regen, Poison Resist)* | **Stacking / Detonation** (*Cinder, Juniper, Nettle*) | Ignores AC/SR; steady whittling damage (`Poison` 1/s per stack, `Burn` 1/0.5s per stack). |
| **Radiant** *(formerly Holy — Rare True Damage)* | **Raw HP / CON / Ward** only | Ignores AC, SR, and stops Undead rising | **Must stay scarce.** Reserved for heavy payoffs (*Sir Dane’s Smite*, *Bram’s Divine Strike*) or stepped-down dice (*Lumen’s `d6` beams*). |

## 5. Standardized Combat Statuses & Keywords

To eliminate text bloat and hidden interactions, every recurring status has one canonical behavior:

- **Burn:** Fire DoT ticking every `0.5s` per stack; decays slowly unless fed or detonated.
- **Poison:** Steady DoT ticking every `1.0s` per stack; never decays; resisted by `CON`.
- **Necrosis:** Grows a visible execution threshold from the left edge of the HP bar; target dies immediately when `HP ≤ Necrosis`.
- **Exposed:** `−2 AC` and `−2 SR` for its duration *(also reveals Stealth)*.
- **Root:** Prevents movement *(does not stop attacking or casting)*.
- **Stun:** Prevents movement, attacking, and casting.
- **Stagger:** Slows attack speed (`−30%`).
- **Taunt:** Forces enemy to target the taunter.
- **Stealth:** Untargetable briefly; drops enemy aggro; breaks on attacking/Exposed.
- **Evasion:** `% chance` to negate incoming **basic attacks** for 0 damage *(multiplicative stacking)*.
- **Hunter’s Mark *(Ranger)*:** `+1d4` bonus damage on every hit *(stacks)*.
- **Hex *(Warlock)*:** Enemy-bound status unique to each Warlock’s Patron (*Infernal Hex*, *Radiant Hex*).
- **Bardic Inspiration *(Bard)*:** Either a **Lore Die** *(adds to next ability d20 roll)* or a **Mote of Potential** *(adds bonus damage to next damaging ability + Gold on kill)*.

## 6. Enemy & Boss Balancing Guidelines

Use this checklist whenever authoring or tuning enemies, elites, and bosses in [`src/02-enemies.js`](../src/02-enemies.js) and [`src/06-adventure.js`](../src/06-adventure.js):

1. **Respect the Cubed AC/SR Band (`8–20`):**
   - **Swarm / Minion foes:** `AC 8–11`, `SR 8–10`, low HP — melt to Cleave, AoE, and fast multi-hitters.
   - **Standard Bruisers / Soldiers:** `AC 12–15`, `SR 10–13`.
   - **Armored Elites / Knights / Golems:** `AC 16–20`, `SR 10–14` — reward **STR Armor Pen**, **Exposed**, **Magic**, or **Radiant**.
   - **Mage / Cultist Elites:** `AC 10–12`, `SR 16–19` — reward **Physical** assassins (*Wallaby, Velith, Lorcan*) and **Backline** reach.
   - **Never** push both `AC > 20` AND `SR > 20` on the same enemy unless it is intentionally a Radiant/DoT puzzle boss.
2. **Give Enemies Real Counterplay Against Evasion & Drain:**
   - Because **Evasion** only dodges **basic attacks**, elites and bosses with clean **telegraphed abilities** (e.g., *Owlbear slam*, *Glasstaff Lightning Bolt*, *Flameskull Fireball*, *Brood Mother webs/Necrosis*) naturally keep high-Evasion comps (*Arthur, Naill*) honest without needing "ignores Evasion" hacks.
   - **Overtime (`28s`, `OT_STEP = 5s`, `−15%` Heal & Ward decay per stack, max `−60%`)** naturally prevents infinite Drain Tank / Ward stalls (*Mother Laurel, Faelan, Bram, Jamison*).
3. **Tune Bosses Around Distinct Mechanical Checks:**
   - **War Band *(Multi-unit frontline + Chargers + Warlord)*:** Tests AoE, Intercept (`STR ≥ +3`), Cleave, and target priority.
   - **Owlbear *(Single-target physical juggernaut)*:** Tests single-target sustain, Taunt/Evasion/Ward, Armor Pen, and stacking **Hunter’s Mark / Radiant Hex** *(note: must never render kill-triggered heroes helpless; Gains’s Gold-chance auto and Nettle’s base Hex damage ensure solo-boss viability)*.
   - **Brood Mother *(Summons + Necrosis + Roots)*:** Tests Ward/CON against Necrosis & Poison, backline reach, and AoE cleave.
4. **Automated Balance Verification Gate:**
   - After any stat, mitigation, or enemy-curve update, run headless full-run persona benchmarks (`Casual`, `Zero-Item`, `Competent`, `Synergy`, `Greed`) and target:
     - **Naive / Zero-Item runs:** `< 15%` Act 3 clear rate *(items and builds matter)*.
     - **Competent / Synergy runs:** `55–70%` Act 3 clear rate with real Piggy Bank tension (`40–65%` break rate).
     - **Greed / Pack 5 runs:** `≤ 70%` clear rate *(high risk/reward, not an automatic 80%+ free win)*.
     - **No single Act 3 boss** should be a free win compared to the others; spread boss lethality evenly across all three threats.
