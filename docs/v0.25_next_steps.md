# Basements & Basilisks v0.25: next steps (24 Nov 2026)

Everything since the v0.25 playtest is designed and sim-checked, not played. The plan is to get it into the playable prototype, so feel can be judged in play.

## Decided today (Luke, 24 Nov)
- **Act 1 maxes at three heroes.** The 4th party slot is bought in the Rousing shop after the Act 1 boss (proposed price about 55 gold plus the hero).
- **Act 2 is sized for two paths:** four heroes, or three heroes with more tier-ups, items and perks.
- **The whole kit works together.** Casts, Class Features, base stats and passives make one fun, readable character. A cast extends or pays off the kit (self-buffs are fine); it doesn't need to swing the fight. The cast-off test only flags a hero the party leans on too hard.
- **Lasting effects start at about half uptime** on any one target for a bare kit; items, perks and stats can push them to permanent. No per-hero caps.

## Done (proposed numbers, sim only)
- Act 1 encounters rebuilt from the enemy template, with Rank 1 resized for a party of three: `docs/v0.25_act1_encounters.md`.
- Cast-rate pass for the 8 heroes in the sim (job-based charge, about 5.6s average): `docs/v0.25_cast_rate_pass.md`.
- Sim: `tools/act1-sim/` (Python; `python3 final.py` for encounters, `python3 metrics.py` for per-hero casts).

## Next, in order
1. **Apply the cast-rate rules to the other 16 heroes.** Attackers: charge per attack = 100 × base attack interval ÷ 6s. Casters: base interval 5s × (1 + 0.1 × INT modifier). Frontliners: +15 per hit taken. Trim durations to the bare-kit uptime baseline. Spot-check each in the sim.
2. **Build the next prototype:** the Act 1 encounters and enemy stats, the three-hero cap with the 4th slot in the Rousing shop, job-based charge, the linear resistance curve (K = 20), earned Last Stand. Port the 0.24 combat feedback (hit numbers, d20 pop-ups, bigger cells), since opaque combat was half the playtest problem. Attacker cast cadence ships at about 6s, with 5s as an easy setting to try.
3. **Re-run the bot tests on the real engine,** starting with Nezznar and King Grol, where the quick sim's strong bot is weakest.
4. **Luke plays it.** What feels off becomes the input for the identity pass: Faelan's centrality, the Kaska rework, and the identity swap test across shared verbs.
