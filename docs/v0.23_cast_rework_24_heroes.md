# Basements & Basilisks v0.23 — Complete 24-Hero Cast Rework

**Status:** Locked Roster Specification (All 6 Packs · 24 Heroes)  
**Design Rules:** Follows [`v0.23_character_design_philosophy.md`](./v0.23_character_design_philosophy.md) and [`v0.23_combat_stats_and_balance_pillars.md`](./v0.23_combat_stats_and_balance_pillars.md).

> **Global Cast Changes Applied Across All 24 Heroes:**
> - **Holy Damage → Radiant Damage** everywhere (and trimmed to rare, deliberate sources).
> - **Paths → Specializations** (strictly ≤ 1 line of text; hidden on Tier I cards until chosen at Tier II).
> - **Per-Hero Tier IV Clauses Removed** → replaced by shared **Tier IV Epic Boons** (pick 1 of 3).
> - **Hero Quests Moved to Quest Board (`❖`)** → opt-in only; zero Quest text on base hero cards.
> - **Evasion** replaces discrete *Alert* / *Afterimage* dodge stacks as a rare universal stat (`% chance to take 0 damage from enemy basic attacks`).

## Pack 1: You Meet at a Tavern

### 1. Arthur — *Human Fighter (Battle Master)*
- **Role / Reach / Pair / Engine:** Damage · Melee (`Range 1`) · `STR / DEX` · Hybrid *(Basic Attacks + On-Hit Ability)*
- **Signature Verb:** **Evade & Counter** *(Slippery frontline Fighter who evades hits with Alert and answers with Action Surge, while Longbow Volley keeps him versatile across item builds.)*
- **Basic Attack:** *Longsword* (`1d8` slashing).
- **Passive *(Compensator)*:** **Alert** — Grants **+20% Evasion**.
- **Class Keyword (*Action Surge*):** Whenever Arthur **evades** an attack, he triggers **Action Surge** *(an immediate free Longsword strike)*.
- **Ability — *Longbow Volley*:** Looses **1 / 2 / 3 / 4** arrows at the farthest foes *(triggers on-hit)*.
  - **High:** Gains **+25% Evasion** *(4s)*.
  - **Nat 20:** Grants the **entire party +35% Evasion** *(5s)*.
- **Specializations *(Tier II, choose 1)*:**
  - **Constant Vigilance:** *Arthur gains +15% Evasion.*
  - **Roll for Initiative:** *Allies gain +25 Charge whenever they evade an attack.*
- **Quest Board — *Flame Gauntlet*:** *Evade 12 attacks* → Replaces **Longbow Volley** with **Gauntlet Blast** *(frontline fire cone + Burn, keeping High/Nat 20 Evasion)*.
- **What Changed from v0.22:** Converted discrete Alert stacks to **Evasion**; removed `<50% HP` Action Surge trigger, starting `+25 Charge`, `+15 Charge` on Surge, `+50%` Surge damage, first-volley Exposed, and ally-Surge Exposed.
