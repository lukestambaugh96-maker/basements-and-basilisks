# Basements & Basilisks v0.25: Cast-rate pass

**Date:** 2026-11-24
**Status:** Proposed. Sim results, nothing implemented. Covers the 8 heroes in the Act 1 sim; the other 16 get the same rules below.
**Builds on:** Design Bible 0.25 (charge locked to job; planned pass 2: "about 5s average at Tier 1"), the hero design session §2.4 (ability share targets: attacker 25%, balanced 40%, caster 65%), the Balance Reference (four knobs; heal loops; support centrality) and the Act 1 encounters doc.

## Result

Average cast interval at T1 is now **about 5.6s**: frontliners and casters 5.0–5.5s, attackers about 6.3s. Every frontliner casts **at least 2 times before falling** in every Act 1 fight (2.2–5.6 casts). Charge now follows jobs only: no time-based trickle for attackers and frontliners.

**Attackers sit at about 6s, not 5s.** At a 5s cadence an attacker's ability shrinks to about one and a half basic attacks to hold the 25% share; at about 6s, shares land at 23–34%. Since casts are now a payoff of the kit rather than a fight-swinger (Luke, 24 Nov), either cadence works; it's a feel call.

## Rules this pass suggests (proposed)

- **Attacker charge per attack = 100 × base attack interval ÷ 6s.** Naill (0.95s) gets about 16 per attack; Pillar (2.4s) about 40. Attack speed then speeds casts too, which is why attack speed counts as two buckets for attackers.
- **Caster base interval = 5s × (1 + 0.1 × INT modifier),** so a typical caster lands near 5s. INT stays the caster stat.
- **Frontliner charge = a flat +15 per hit taken.** Flat per hit, not per damage, so bigger enemy hits don't make heals come faster (the heal-loop risk). Swarms feed it more; that's a frontliner's reward for standing in one.
- **Durations shrink with faster casts.** Uptime = duration ÷ interval, so any ability that lasts (dodge, Twin Shot, Inspire, taunt) gains uptime as casts speed up. **Baseline for a bare kit:** uptime on any one target at about half or less. Arthur's old 1.5s dodge was up 32% of the fight at a 5s cadence. **This is a starting point, not a cap (Luke, 24 Nov):** items, perks and stats can push any lasting effect to permanent uptime. That's an engine the player builds toward.

## What the sim showed

Typical parties, Fights 2–5, 200 drafts per fight. "Win with / without" turns that one hero's ability off. **This is a centrality check, not a requirement:** casts don't need to swing fights (Luke, 24 Nov); a big drop only flags a hero the whole party leans on.

| Hero | Casts per fight | Win with / without ability |
|---|---|---|
| Faelan | 6.7 | 54% / 5% |
| Arthur | 3.4 | 73% / 17% |
| Bram | 4.4 | 49% / 15% |
| Zark | 3.3 | 64% / 22% |
| Wallaby | 3.6 | 52% / 25% |
| Lorcan | 3.7 | 53% / 29% |
| Rosalind | 4.7 | 37% / 25% |
| Sir Dane | 3.1 | 35% / 29% |

- **Faelan carries her parties.** Without Shield, they almost never win. That's the Balance Reference's support-centrality risk, and her ward was already cut by a die. Worth a look in the identity pass.
- **Sir Dane's and Rosalind's casts change win rates little.** That's fine under the new framing: a taunt or a speed buff is a payoff of their kits. Whether they feel distinct is a question for the identity swap test, not for their numbers.
- Enemies were retuned after this pass (Rank 1 HP about −10%); see the encounters doc. Every Act 1 fight is back in range.
