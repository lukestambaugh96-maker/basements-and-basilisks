# Basements & Basilisks v0.23 — Item Architecture & Complete Catalog

**Status:** Locked Item Specification (58 Universal Playstyle Items + 24 Unique Class-Keyword Items = 82 Total Items)  
**Design Rules:** Follows [`v0.23_character_design_philosophy.md`](./v0.23_character_design_philosophy.md), [`v0.23_combat_stats_and_balance_pillars.md`](./v0.23_combat_stats_and_balance_pillars.md), [`v0.23_cast_rework_24_heroes.md`](./v0.23_cast_rework_24_heroes.md), and the balance lessons from [`v0.22_meta_report_and_lessons.md`](./v0.22_meta_report_and_lessons.md).

> **North Star:** *Every item fits a clear playstyle, every name is iconic D&D, and rarity scales both power and complexity cleanly.* Commons and Uncommons carry **zero effect text**; Rare, Very Rare, Legendary, and Unique items carry **at most one short sentence**.

## 1. The Six-Tier Item Architecture

The item pool is cleanly divided into **5 Universal Playstyle Tiers** (sold in standard post-fight shops and Treasure chests) plus a dedicated **`Unique` Tier** (purchased from a **Special Vendor** map node):

| Tier | Ink Color | Cost | Design Rule | Where Found |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Common** *(12)* | `#4d483d` | **2g** | **1** minor Ability Score or **1** minor Combat Stat *(0 effect text; also Zoe’s `Rummage` pool)*. | Standard Shop *(all Acts)*, Zoe `Rummage` |
| **Uncommon** *(14)* | `#226d3a` | **3g** | **2** different Ability Scores or Combat Stats *(0 effect text; disparate playstyle pairings)*. | Standard Shop *(all Acts)*, Treasure |
| **Rare** *(12)* | `#12477d` | **4g** | Larger stat *(or 2 middling)* + **1 concise minor effect** *(no on-hit bigger than `1d4`)*. | Standard Shop *(scales up per encounter)*, Treasure |
| **Very Rare** *(12)* | `#4b127d` | **6g** | Upper-end stats + **1 crisp endgame build-enabler**. | Standard Shop *(Act 2–3)*, Treasure |
| **Legendary** *(8)* | `#8a4a0e` | **10g** | Top stats + **1 borderline-gamebreaking** iconic D&D artifact effect. | Late Act 3 Shop *(ultra-rare)*, Act 2–3 Boss / Treasure |
| **Unique** *(24)* | `#1b7a78` | **5g** | **Class-Keyword items** *(2 per class: 1 Trigger/Resource Gain + 1 Modifier)* with stats. | **Special Vendor** map node *(filtered to party Class Keywords)* |

### Standing Item Guardrails (v0.23)
1. **1 Line Maximum:** No multi-clause paragraphs. Commons and Uncommons have zero effect text.
2. **Exact Per-Tier 6-Attribute Parity:** Every standard tier (`Common` `+1`, `Uncommon` `+2`, `Rare` `+3`, `Very Rare` `+4`, `Legendary` `+6`) gives the exact same total modifier budget to `STR`, `DEX`, `CON`, `INT`, `WIS`, and `CHA` (`+16` each across the 58-item shop pool).
3. **Cubed Mitigation Bounded Accuracy (`K = 14`):** AC and SR bonuses stay tight (`+1` to `+3` max on any single item) so units remain inside the `10–20` bounded-accuracy curve while **Armor Pen**, **Spell Pen**, **Evasion**, and **HP Regen** serve as real counter-stats.
4. **Radiant Damage Stays Scarce:** Formerly *Holy*, **Radiant** true damage is reserved for deliberate high-tier payoffs (*Sun Blade*, *Robe of Stars*, *Holy Avenger*) — never granted cheaply on Commons/Uncommons.
5. **Engine Safety:** Every reactive item trigger carries an explicit `≥ 1.0s` internal cooldown; item heals always pass `{ raw: 1 }` when pre-scaled (never `u.dm²`); no item stacks infinite uncapped `% Max HP/s` sustain.
6. **All Items Always Unlocked:** Standard shop items never lock behind enabled/disabled Hero Packs.
7. **Gilthrax Rarity Value Scaling:** Gilthrax’s *Arcane Shield* and *Gilded Hoard* scale cleanly with item rarity/price (`Common 2g → Uncommon 3g → Rare 4g → Unique 5g → Very Rare 6g → Legendary 10g`).

## 3. The `Unique` Tier — 24 Class-Keyword Items (`5g`, Special Vendor Node)

Purchased exclusively from a **Special Vendor** (*Curio / Relic Merchant*) map node rather than cluttering standard post-fight shops.

- **Exact 2-Per-Class Pairing (All 12 Classes):**
  1. **Trigger / Resource Gain Item:** Generates additional procs or resources for the class keyword (*"Trigger X when..."* / *"Gain X when..."*).
  2. **Modifier Item:** Mutates or empowers what happens whenever the class keyword fires (*"Your X also..."* / *"Holder’s X now..."*).
- **Paladin Focus:** Both Paladin Uniques focus on **`Smite`** *(working cleanly for both Sir Dane’s Divine Smite and Mother Laurel’s Verdant Smite)*.
- **Party-Aware Vendor Stock:** Visiting a Special Vendor offers **3 Unique items drawn from Class Keywords present in your current party** *(avoiding off-class dead offers; if your party already owns all matching Uniques, fills remaining slots with random Uniques or Rare/Very Rare items)*.
- **Stats Included:** Each Unique carries a clean, class-appropriate stat line alongside its 1-line Trigger or Modifier effect.

| Class · Keyword | Trigger / Resource Gain (`5g`) | Modifier (`5g`) |

## 4. Shop Progression & Special Vendor Map Node

### Standard Shop Item Rarity Curve (Smooth Per-Encounter Scaling)
Evaluated by encounters won (`e = run.stats.won`). Commons remain findable into Act 3 while Rare, Very Rare, and ultra-rare **Legendary** artifacts climb smoothly:

| Progress | Common (`2g`) | Uncommon (`3g`) | Rare (`4g`) | Very Rare (`6g`) | Legendary (`10g`) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Start (`e = 0`, Shop #1)** | 65% | 35% | 0% | 0% | 0% |
| **Mid Act 1 (`e = 2`)** | 56% | 36% | 8% | 0% | 0% |
| **Late Act 1 (`e = 4`)** | 46% | 38% | 16% | 0% | 0% |
| **Early Act 2 (`e = 6`)** | 36% | 36% | 23% | 5% | 0% |
| **Mid Act 2 (`e = 8`)** | 28% | 33% | 27% | 12% | 0% |
| **Early Act 3 (`e = 10`)** | 20% | 27% | 30% | 20% | **3%** |
| **Mid Act 3 (`e = 12`)** | 15% | 22% | 30% | 27% | **6%** |
| **Late Act 3 (`e = 15`)** | 10% | 18% | 30% | 33% | **9%** |

- **Treasure Chests (`◆`):** Evaluate the standard curve at `e + 3` *(making Act 2–3 chests an exciting source of Very Rare and early Legendary drops)*.
- **Special Vendor Map Node (`✦` / `⚖` Curio Merchant):**
  - Appears **1–2 times per Act** on branching map paths (giving players an intentional routing choice vs. Training / Campfire / Quest Board / Elite).
  - Stocks **3 `Unique` items (`5g` each)** filtered to the **Class Keywords present in your current party**, with 1 optional reroll (`2g`).
  - Allows players to intentionally hunt the Trigger or Modifier piece that completes their class engine without diluting the regular shop pool.
