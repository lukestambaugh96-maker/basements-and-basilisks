## 1. What Changed Across the 4 Pillars

### Pillar 1: Soft Lanes (`Breach & Flank`) & 13 Redesigned Maps
- **Geometry & Lane Helpers ([`src/01-geo.js`](../src/01-geo.js)):** Added `LANES`, `mirrorLanes()`, `laneOfCol()`, `laneOfUnit()`, and `laneDeployCells()`.
- **13 Maps & 35 Encounters Redesigned ([`src/02-enemies.js`](../src/02-enemies.js)):**
  - All 13 `BATTLE_MAPS` became 2-Lane (`10×10`) or 3-Lane (`11×10`) arenas with terrain dividers across rows `2..6` and open crossover bridges on rows `0..1` and `8..9`.
  - Added the 6-objective `LANE_OBJ` registry: **Main Breach (`⚔`)**, **Dark Ritual (`☽`, 12s)**, **Supply Cart (`💰`, 14s)**, **Duel Choke (`🛡`, 1 Hero max)**, **Battle Shrine (`✦`)**, and **Boss Throne (`♛`)**.
  - Updated all 35 `ENC` spawn layouts to align cleanly with lane corridors and mirrored both grids and lanes cleanly in `buildEncounter()`.
- **Soft-Lane Combat AI & Drag UX ([`src/05-engine.js`](../src/05-engine.js), [`src/10-board.js`](../src/10-board.js)):**
  - Units prioritized enemies in their own lane (`+22` cross-lane target penalty while active foes remained in lane). Winning a lane triggered **Flank Rush** (`+35%` move speed, `+20%` attack speed for `5s`) and let winners cross the open bridges to flank surviving lanes (and **Expose** Bosses).
  - Dropping a 2nd hero onto a **Duel Choke** (`maxHeroes: 1`) lane cleanly swapped with the existing occupant.

### Pillar 2: 36 Shop-Offered House Rules (6-Slot Cap) & Exponential Act 3 Curve
- **House Rules Catalog ([`src/04b-house-rules.js`](../src/04b-house-rules.js)):** Created **36 party-wide permanent modifiers** inspired by *Guildrun* relics, capped at **`HOUSE_RULE_MAX = 6`** slots (with 50% refund sell & replace modals):
  - **13 Class-Keyword Multiplicative Engines** (1 per keyword, supercharged by **Multiclass `✧`** dips).
  - **8 Composition, Lane & d20 Rules** (e.g., *Motley Crew*, *Flank & Conquer*, *Hold the Line*, *Hot Dice Rule*, *Merciful DM*).
  - **6 Economy & Scaling Rules** (e.g., *Guild Treasury*, *Quartermaster's Tab*, *Long-Term Investment*, *Compound Coffers*).
  - **9 Legendary Capstones** (e.g., *Rule of Cool*, *Gestalt Characters*, *Magic Item Hoard*, *Death Saves Off*, *Action Economy*).
- **Boss & Miniboss Drafts + Full Combat Wiring ([`src/05-engine.js`](../src/05-engine.js), [`src/06-adventure.js`](../src/06-adventure.js), [`src/11-shell.js`](../src/11-shell.js)):** Every House Rule was wired into live combat, shop odds, and 1-of-3 Boss/Miniboss Draft rewards (`hrDraftModal`).
- **Piecewise Exponential Difficulty Curve ([`src/06-adventure.js`](../src/06-adventure.js)):** Replaced the flat curve in `curveAt(d)` with a *Balatro*-style 3-Act ramp — gentle Act 1 onboarding (`0.74× → 1.25× HP`), accelerating Act 2 (`1.25× → 3.12× HP`), and steep exponential Act 3 (`3.12× → 7.6×+ HP`).

### Pillar 3: Immediate Power vs. Future Power Camp Shop Economy
- **Camp Rank (`I → IV`) ([`src/06-adventure.js`](../src/06-adventure.js), [`src/11-shell.js`](../src/11-shell.js)):** Invest gold (`7g / 11g / 15g`, discounted **`-1g` per win**) to raise Gold Interest cap (`+5g → +7g → +9g → +12g`, `+4g` more with *Guild Treasury*), unlock Rare/Legendary House Rules & a 3rd Rule slot, boost Tier II/III Hero & Rare+ Item odds, and earn a free reroll per visit at Rank IV.
- **6 `🌱 Scaling` Investment Items ([`src/04-items.js`](../src/04-items.js)):** Added 6 Uncommon scaling items (1 per attribute to preserve exact 6-attribute parity — **88 total items** = 64 standard + 24 unique) that permanently stacked after every combat win while equipped: *Growing Whetstone*, *Quicksilver Vial*, *Heartwood Seed*, *Tome of Compounding*, *Owl's Astrolabe*, and *Ledger of the Mint*.

### Pillar 4: Honest Challenge Rating (`CR`) & Post-Combat Autopsy
- **Deterministic CR & Lane Diagnostics ([`src/06-adventure.js`](../src/06-adventure.js), [`src/11-shell.js`](../src/11-shell.js)):**
  - Live **Party CR** badge (`CR X.X ×Engine`) across Shop, Map, and Combat headers.
  - Pre-scouted **Map Node CR & Lane Count** (`CR 3 · 2L`, `CR 14 · DEADLY`) color-coded live against Party CR (`Favoured` / `Even` / `Dangerous` / `Deadly`).
  - Live **Deployment Lane Headers** aligned above each lane showing `Hero CR vs Enemy CR`, objective rules/timers, and tactical flags (`⚠ Uncontested`, `⚠ High AC`, `★ Ally Link active`).
- **Post-Combat Autopsy ([`src/11-shell.js`](../src/11-shell.js)):** Every fight summary broke down **Lane Results**, **House Rule Engine contributions**, **Scaling growth notes**, **AC/SR mitigation warnings** (when enemies blocked ≥30% of physical/magical damage), and a **d20 Luck Audit** (average roll vs. `10.5` expected, High rolls, Nat 20s, and Fumbles).
