### Pack 4: Divine Intervention (Reworked Reference)
- **Pillar** *(Goliath Zealot Barbarian · Tank · STR/STR)*: **Monument Charge** — High triggers **Zealot Rage** (cannot drop below 1 HP for 2s); Nat 20 cracks the ground in a line Stun + Holy burst. *V: Unstoppable Faith / S: Living Beacon (taking hits gives allies Holy).* **Quest — Living Colossus:** Becomes Large (2x2) stone statue (+3 STR, wider Nat 20 shockwave).
- **Mother Laurel** *(Elf Ancients Paladin · Tank · Melee Reach 2 · STR/WIS)*: **Nature's Wrath** — Sweeping glaive + Root; High blooms healing/cleanse; Nat 20 Roots all melee foes + party HoT/Ward. *V: Ancients' Ward (WIS Spell Resistance aura) / S: Bountiful Vines (Rooted foes take bonus Holy).* **Quest — Garden of Plenty:** Fruit/blossoms buff party sustain.
- **Lumen** *(Aasimar Light Cleric · Damage · Ranged · WIS/WIS)*: **Sunbeam** — Line Holy + **Sear**; High awakens **Radiant Soul** (piercing Holy auto-attacks); Nat 20 double-width beam detonating all Sear/Burn. *V: Judgement (+1 d20 per Sear) / S: Purifying Light (Holy applies Sear; Sear counts as Burn).* **Quest — Book of Judgement:** Scaling radiant execution.
- **Brother Jamison** *(Human Peace Cleric · Support · Melee · CHA/CHA)*: **Emboldening Bond** — Links allies for `d4` d20 bonus; High links 3 + pulses *Word of Radiance*; Nat 20 bonds whole party (any High/20 feeds Charge to all bonded allies). *V: Good Vibes (shared damage + WIS floor) / S: Peace & Radiance (ally casts trigger free Holy pulses).* **Quest — Holy Symbol of Pelor:** Bonded allies share Jamison's WIS d20 floor.

### Pack 5: Cornering the Market (Reworked Reference)
- **Zoe** *(Human Rune Knight Fighter · Support · Melee · STR/INT)*: **Rummage** — Tosses temp buff/potion; High gives **Runic Upgrade** (+50% item stats for the fight); Nat 20 refreshes once-per-fight items + Gold. *V: Rune Carver / S: Just the Thing (2+ items = INT + on-hit sparks).* **Quest — Grand Opening:** +2 INT, High pulls 2 buffs, +1 shop item slot at 20% off.
- **Toot** *(Plasmoid Creation Bard · Support · Ranged · CHA/CHA)*: Attack earns Gold. **Fanfare (Motes)** — High sends 2 Motes (Advantage on next cast) + Gold; Nat 20 gives whole party Motes + double fight Gold. *V: Busker's Rhythm / S: Street Performer (ally High/20s drop Gold).* **Quest — Record Deal:** Amplifies Gold scaling and Mote buffs.
- **Gilthrax** *(Gold Dragonborn Eldritch Knight · Tank · Melee · STR/CON)*: **Arcane Shield** — High raises huge item-scaling Ward + 100% reflect; Nat 20 AoE Arcane Nova + Gold. *V: Gilded Hoard (+AC and +1 d20 per item) / S: Bodyguard's Fee (reflects feed Charge/Gold).* **Quest — Gems, Glorious Gems:** Can "eat" cheap shop items to permanently absorb their stats without using slots.
- **Gains** *(Tiefling Transmutation Wizard · Damage · Ranged · INT/INT)*: **Gilded Beam** — High applies **Gilded** (slow; drops Gold on death); Nat 20 scales off unspent Gold and Gilds neighbours. *V: Compound Interest (+1 d20 per 10 Gold held) / S: Liquid Assets (spending Gold grants Charge speed).* **Quest — Compound Interest:** 10% end-of-fight Gold interest (cap +5g) + permanent INT scaling from total Gold earned.

### Pack 6: Surprise Round (Reworked Reference)
- **Velith** *(Drow Assassin Rogue · Damage · Melee · DEX/DEX)*: **Cloak of Darkness** — High deals heavy burst + Poison + re-Stealth; Nat 20 executes and chains to a second foe. *V: Death's Ledger (opening Advantage + Stealth d20 bonus) / S: Nightshade Vials (Poison + opening-5s burst).* **Quest — Death's Ledger:** Permanent kill-tracking scaling.
- **Corvin** *(Human Gloom Stalker Ranger · Damage · Melee · DEX/WIS)*: **Dread Ambush** — High fires 3 rapid on-hit strikes + Umbral Sight (untargetable 2s) + Marked; Nat 20 spawns a shadow echo repeating the spin. *V: Stalker's Flurry / S: First Strike (first-4s casts grant INT + Marked).* **Quest — Twin Blades of the Gloom:** +2 DEX, third afterimage on basics, shadow echo triggers on High rolls.
- **Saint** *(Half-Elf Trickery Cleric · Support · Ranged · WIS/CHA)*: **Toll the Dead** — Necrotic bell + Illusory Double; High applies Necrosis + Double mimics cast; Nat 20 redirects enemy backline + steals buff + heals. *V: Two-Faced (Double detonates for Necrosis + Charge) / S: Smoke & Mirrors (ally dodges/Alert/Stealth spawn decoys + +2 d20).* **Quest — Mirror Image:** +2 CHA, starts with a Double, ally Nat 20s spawn free Doubles.
- **Kaska** *(Half-Orc Moon Druid · Support · Melee · STR/WIS)*: **Moonlit Pounce** — Starts in **Panther form** (leaps + Exposed; shifts to Half-Orc Thorn Whip when Panther HP breaks); High heals/Stealths or re-shifts to Panther; Nat 20 Stuns + party moonlight buff. *V: Lunar Resilience / S: Pack Hunt (allies hitting Exposed foes heal + gain Charge).* **Quest — Moon's Favour:** Empowers Panther form and moonlight aura.

## Part 2: Future Pack Concepts

### Mechanical Gap Packs (Unused Design Bible Elements)
1. **Eye of the Storm** *(Lightning Charge, Thunder Detonation & Push)* — Sky-priests and storm-chasers stacking Lightning Charge and detonating it with Thunder knockbacks. *Pack Item: Javelin of Lightning.*
2. **Cold Shoulder** *(Cold / Freeze & Frontline Walls)* — Winter expeditionists stacking attack-speed slows into Freeze stuns and shatter payoffs; adds more dedicated Tanks. *Pack Item: Frost Brand.*
3. **Grave Mistake** *(Summons, Necrosis, Acid & Rot)* — Temporary combat minions, Necrosis, and Acid armor-shredding. *Pack Item: Alchemist's Acid.*
4. **Hold My Ale** *(Forced Movement, Throws & 1v1 Duels)* — Tavern bouncers and grapplers throwing tanks into backlines and isolating duels. *Pack Item: Gauntlets of Ogre Power.*
5. **Fresh Off the Cart** *(Novice Hyper-Scalers)* — Reimagined "Hopefuls" with fast quests that peak by Act 2 and scale dynamically off teammates. *Pack Item: Adventurer's Journal.*

### Thematic & Cultural Packs
6. **Magic Academy ("Dean's List" / Strixhaven)** — Pure INT Charge-speed, spell preparation, and weird casting rules.
7. **Main Character Syndrome** — Hyper-competitive anime rivals, ego mechanics, and Exodia-style stat quests.
8. **Warband** *(Goblin, Bugbear, Hobgoblin)* — Tactical goblinoid hierarchy: Hobgoblin Side-by-side commander, Bugbear reach-2 Ambush opener, Goblin Stealth nuisance, Nilbog d20 chaos.
9. **High Seas, High Stakes (Pirates)** — Bridges Gold, Ambush, and Water/Push (boarding hooks, cursed doubloons, cannon volleys).
10. **Ancestry Cultural Packs** *(All-Dwarf / All-Elf / All-Dragonborn)* — Built around cultural engines rather than trait-counting: *Forge & Anvil* (Dwarves: items/armor), *Fey Grace* (Elves: dodge/Ward/d20), *Draconic Hoard* (Dragonborn: multi-element + Gold/stat scaling).

## Part 3: Backlog of Individual Hero Concepts

### Magic Academy Pack Candidates
- **Magic Missile Wizard** *(Damage · INT/INT)*: Auto-hitting Magic Missile basic attacks; casts permanently add missiles for the fight; Nat 20 gives orbiting on-hit missiles to all allies.
- **Wand of Wonder Wizard** *(Support/Damage · INT/WIS)*: Rolls on a wild spell table; stacking **WIS (d20 floor)** trims duds/backfires off the bottom of the table to guarantee jackpots.
- **Prepared Spells Wizard** *(Support · INT/INT)*: Pre-combat toggle between 3–4 spell loadouts (Ward, AoE Burn, Root, Nuke) to counter specific enemy cards.
- **Elf Necromancer + Growing Homunculus** *(Support/Tank · INT/CON)*: Starts with a tiny Homunculus summon that grafts parts on enemy deaths / High rolls, growing into a Large (2x2) monster.

### Main Character Syndrome Pack Candidates
- **"Pride" — Hellfire Tiefling Barbarian** *(Damage · STR/CON)*: Vegeta/Kaiba antihero. Starts with an **Ego** buff (auto-High rolls, burns own HP for Hellfire). Dropping below 50% HP without a kill shatters Ego ("Pride before the fall")—unless he lands a Nat 20 while humbled for an unstoppable comeback state.
- **Platinum Dragon Teen Paladin** *(Tank/Damage · STR/CHA)*: Hyper-competitive #1 chaser. Reads the combat damage meter: gains massive INT and +2 d20 while behind an ally in damage; radiates a party-wide Platinum Holy aura while in #1st place.
- **Tiamat's Avatar Dragonborn** *(Damage/Tank · All Stats)*: Exodia-style quest to reach a high threshold in all 6 attributes; transforms into a Large (2x2) 5-headed Avatar applying all 5 elemental statuses on High/Nat 20.
- **"Bump, Set, Spike" Brawler** *(Support/Damage · STR/DEX)*: 3-beat combo loop—**Bump** (parry to gain Charge), **Set** (launch enemy, Exposed), **Spike** (when any ally hits the Set foe, slams them for AoE shockwave).

### Wildcard Candidates
- **Half-Orc "Cop" Paladin** *(Tank · STR/WIS · City Watch / Hold My Ale)*: Premier anti-diver tank who arrests whichever enemy moved farthest/jumped the backline, rooting and cuffing (silencing/disarming) them on High rolls.
- **Half-Elf Fire Sorcerer + Tiefling Fire Elemental Wife** *(Damage · CHA/CHA · Elemental / Power Couples)*: Two-unit hero; ranged caster + melee fire elemental frontline. His High/Nat 20 triggers a tag-team dash where she detonates every Burned enemy on the board.
- **Dark Elf "Wine" Ranger** *(Damage/Support · DEX/WIS · Pirates / Grave Mistake)*: Arrows apply **Vintage** (Poison + "Tipsy" miss chance); High ferments Poison for burst; Nat 20 pops the cork across the whole enemy team.
